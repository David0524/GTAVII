# AUDIO_SOURCES: provenance of every sound

**Rule (from the brief):** no real recorded music, no commercial sample libraries, no ripped game or
film audio, no imitation of real voices, stations, jingles or advertisers. Only our own synthesis,
our own procedural content, or CC0/public-domain recordings, each with a row here.

**Status (2026-11-26): every sound in the game is synthesised by code in this repository, and the
speech is rendered offline with eSpeak NG.** No third-party recording, sample, impulse response or
voice model is used anywhere. There is nothing to attribute and no licence other than the project's own.

## Sound generated at runtime (no files)

| What | Where | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Car, taxi, truck and bus engines | `worklets/processors.ts` `t74-engine`, profiles in `worklets/engineProfile.ts` | firing pulses per cylinder (real firing orders and bank layout), exhaust waveguides, muffler, resonances, intake, valvetrain, diesel knock, Roots-blower whine |
| Traffic mass | `t74-traffic` | granular cloud of engine wavetables + road and wet-tyre noise |
| Rain, drips | `t74-rain` | modal synthesis: Minnaert bubbles (van den Doel's model), metal pings, canvas thumps, pavement ticks |
| Crowds, kids in parks; fallback voice for lines not yet rendered | `t74-walla`, `t74-voice` | formant synthesis (glottal source, three formants), syllable scheduler; no words, no real voice |
| Reverb, early reflections | `t74-fdn`, `t74-early` | 8-line FDN and listener-centric multi-tap reflections driven by the real building footprints |
| Radio tuning static | `t74-static` | noise, crackle, heterodyne whistles |
| Radio music (procedural period-style instrumentals) | `t74-music` → `worklets/music.ts` | generative arranger (key, progression, form, motif per song seed) + synthesised drums, bass, FM keys, Karplus-Strong guitar, strings, brass, lead; tape bus. Song titles/artists are the narrative's invented ones |
| Horns, sirens, skids, subway gratings, steam, AC units, hum, roar, wind, aircraft | `synth/live.ts` | Web Audio oscillators, periodic waves, filtered noise |
| One-shots (doors, impacts, glass, brakes, relays, manholes, carts, docks, footsteps, birds, bells, fireworks, ship horn, compactor, jackhammer, power up/down, UI, phone bell) | `synth/bake.ts` (baked in a worker at load) | modal synthesis, filtered noise, FM |

Generic period *types* (an electronic wail siren, a dual-tone disc horn, a two-stroke diesel with a
Roots blower, a Bell-style telephone ringer, a bronze church bell) are modelled from physical
principles; no specific product's recording or sound signature is copied.

## Source decisions (user, 2026-11-26: "use free methods for now; generate speech locally")
- **Music:** procedural period-style instrumentals, generated in the browser (`worklets/music.ts`).
- **Voice:** eSpeak NG, rendered offline at build time (`npm run audio:voices`, needs the `espeak-ng`
  package). Chosen over neural TTS because its voices contain no recording of a real person and carry
  no dataset-licence questions; it sounds robotic, which is the cost. Lines without a render (new or
  edited text) fall back to the formant placeholder until re-rendered.
Any recorded file added later gets a row in the table above before it is committed.
