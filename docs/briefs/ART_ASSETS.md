# Design: true-to-life graphics (photoscanned materials and the user's image-generation pipeline)

The user: *"Graphics should really be true to life. I can handle a ton of ChatGPT image prompts if
necessary for signs, branding, etc."* (They said it can be a later solution; parts of it are cheap now.)

## Lever 1 (now): CC0 photoscanned materials and lighting
Facades, ground and interiors are fully procedural today. Procedural detail can be stylish, but it
reads as CG next to the 1974 photographs. **Layer real photoscanned PBR materials under the
procedural systems:**
- **Sources:** CC0 libraries only. **Poly Haven** (textures and HDRIs) and **ambientCG** are both
  CC0, so they're free to ship with no attribution needed, though credit them anyway. Record each
  file with its URL and licence in `docs/ART_SOURCES.md`.
- **What to take:** period-plausible brick (common red, glazed, painted), brownstone, limestone and
  granite, cast iron, terrazzo, asphalt (worn, patched, wet), concrete sidewalk with expansion
  joints, granite curbs, cobbles, tar roofing, rusted steel, chipped enamel paint, plaster, linoleum,
  tile (subway, hex), wood floors, marble. **No modern materials** (the STYLE_GUIDE anachronism
  checklist applies).
- **How:** the procedural layout stays (window grids, coursing, condition-driven soot and wear), and
  the scans supply micro-detail and albedo variation through triplanar or atlas sampling, with the
  condition grade (src/shared/condition.ts) blending between clean and grimy variants. Detail tiling
  is broken up with macro variation.
- **HDRIs** (Poly Haven, CC0) as reference and fill for sky, reflections and probes, graded to the
  1974 look. Vehicles need a city `scene.environment` anyway.
- **Budgets:** KTX2/Basis-compressed, mipmapped, streamed by chunk. Texture memory ≤ 256 MB on High
  and ≤ 64 MB on mobile Low (half-resolution sets on Low). Measure on the user's phone.
- **Owners:** engine (facades, ground, streets, the shared material library), interiors (interior
  surfaces from the same library), vehicles (tyres, interiors, grime), characters (fabric detail
  maps).

## Lever 2 (later, user-generated): image prompts for signs, branding and printed matter
The user will generate images in ChatGPT from **prompt packs** we write. This suits art that must
look hand-made and period-true and can't come from scans:
- **Good fits:** storefront signs and painted window lettering, neon sign artwork, billboards and
  painted wall ads, posters and handbills, cinema marquee and lobby cards for invented films,
  newspaper photos and front-page art, product packaging on shelves, menus, matchbooks, the folding
  map's cover, the title card, record sleeves (shelved until radio resumes), graffiti tags,
  magazine covers, and pinned photos in homes (Delphine's prints).
- **Rules every prompt carries:**
  1. 1974 Manhattan period style and materials, fitting the STYLE_GUIDE palette.
  2. **No real brands, logos, trademarks or real people's likenesses.** Only names from narrative's
     registries (BUSINESS_NAMES.md, businesses.json, the gun and vehicle registries).
  3. **Exact text, or no text.** AI image text can garble, so prompts either give the exact string
     to render and we verify it letter by letter, or ask for **no text**, and our renderer sets the
     words in the period fonts (better for anything a mission reads).
  4. A fixed aspect ratio and size per slot, and a transparent or flat background where the asset
     is a cut-out.
  5. Flat, frontal and evenly lit (we light it in-engine), unless the slot is a photo.
- **The pack format** (`assets/prompts/<pack>.md`): one row per asset with an id, target file name,
  where it's used, size and aspect, the exact prompt to paste, text policy, and acceptance notes.
  Packs come in batches of about 20 so the user can work through them on their phone.
- **Getting images back:** the user uploads them with the given file names to
  `assets/incoming/<pack>/` (GitHub's web upload works from a phone) or attaches them to the owning
  chat. An **ingest tool** (`tools/assets/ingest.ts`) then validates names and sizes, crops and
  cleans backgrounds, grades to the 1974 look, compresses to KTX2/WebP, packs atlases and registers
  the asset. A person (the owning chat, then my sweep) checks each image for garbled text, modern or
  real-brand content, and anachronisms before it ships.
- **Licensing note for the user:** OpenAI's terms give the user ownership of their outputs. Keep a
  record in `docs/ART_SOURCES.md` (pack, date, "generated by the project owner with ChatGPT").
- **Owners:** engine (the asset spec per slot, the ingest pipeline, in-world placement), narrative
  (prompt text from its registries, with exact copy), UI (title, map and paper assets), interiors
  (shop interiors, posters, menus, packaging), oversight (the quality gate).
- **One place (user instruction):** every image prompt from every chat goes into a single generated master queue,
  `assets/prompts/ALL_PROMPTS.md` plus `prompts.json`, owned by narrative. Other chats send their prompt requests to narrative.
  The user runs the whole queue in one batch later, using **Claude in Chrome** driving ChatGPT's image tool, so the queue
  carries an unattended runbook (paste, check, retry, save with the exact filename, upload to `assets/incoming/`).
- **Timing:** write the first prompt pack (the 40 storefronts of the vertical slice's blocks plus
  the Deuce marquees) once Phase 3 facades have sign slots; the user starts generating when ready.
