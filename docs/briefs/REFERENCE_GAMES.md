# Reference games for everything (user decision, 2026-11-27)

> User: "Reference those games for EVERYTHING" (GTA V, GTA VI, RDR2), and "Also reference 1970s NYC".

## The rule
Every system, screen, sound, mission and asset is benchmarked against **GTA V, GTA VI (public footage
and trailers) and RDR2** before it's called done. 1970s New York (photos, film, period sources)
decides *what* exists; the three games decide *how well* it's done, how it feels, and how deep it goes.

### Originality guardrails (non-negotiable)
- Study **behaviour, structure, feel, quality and depth**. Never copy assets, code, animations, audio,
  UI art, logos, fonts, names, characters, mission scripts, dialogue or distinctive look-alike designs.
- No real brands or real people's likenesses. Our names and art stay original (see the landmark rename rules).
- Comments and docs may name the games as references; nothing player-facing may.

## What each stream benchmarks

| Stream | Benchmark in GTA V / VI / RDR2 |
|---|---|
| **Engine / look** | Time-of-day lighting, GI, reflections, wet streets, fog, night city density, LOD stability, streaming without pop-in, draw distance feel (VISUAL_BAR.md) |
| **Movement** | On-foot weight, stepping, traversal, ragdoll, camera (MOVEMENT.md) |
| **Vehicles** | Handling feel per class, weight transfer, damage model, deformation, wheel and suspension behaviour, crash physics, car audio hooks, enter, exit and carjack |
| **Sim (NPCs and traffic)** | Ambient pedestrian life and scenarios, reactions to the player, traffic behaviour and honking, accidents, police AI and pursuit tactics, the RDR2 level of NPC memory and greetings |
| **Characters** | Faces, skin, hair, clothing, wardrobe variety, facial animation, idle variety, the RDR2 level of animation detail |
| **Gameplay** | Gunplay feel (aim assist on touch, recoil, hit reactions), cover, wanted/heat flow (our witnesses and call boxes, not stars), stealing cars, death and arrest flow, economy depth (GTA V/VI money-making variety), weapon and shop flow, stamina, health |
| **Missions and story (narrative)** | Mission structure, checkpointing, variety per mission, set pieces, character arcs, side-quest strangeness (GTA strangers and freaks, RDR2 strangers), radio-free dialogue during drives, cutscene pacing |
| **Interiors** | Interior density and storytelling clutter, lighting quality, entry and exit transitions (no loading screens where avoidable) |
| **UI, map and cinematics** | HUD minimalism, minimap and discovery, phone/pause/map flow, menus over the world, the RDR2 level of diegetic polish, cutscene camera language, letterboxing, title and loading screens |
| **Audio** | Ambience layering by block and time, footsteps by surface, gun reports and canyon echo, vehicle engines, crowd walla, police dispatch, mix and ducking, score cues |

## How it's checked
Every milestone report and every shot board includes a short **"vs GTA V / VI / RDR2"** section:
- what the reference does;
- where we match it;
- where we fall short, with a plan or an honest "won't reach on the phone".

Oversight rejects milestones without that section, and still requires the 1974 reference compare for
period content.
