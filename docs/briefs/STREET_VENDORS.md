# Street vendors (user decision, 2026-11-27)

> User: "Just realized we're absolutely going to need street vendors."

Today the sim has a single generic `npc.vendor` (white apron, one `vendor_cart` prop). That's not enough.
1974 Midtown sidewalks were full of vendors and they're core to the street-life bar (VISUAL_BAR "Life",
REFERENCE_GAMES: GTA V/VI ambient scenarios, RDR2 vendors and stalls). All brands are invented.

## Vendor types (June 1974, Midtown)
- Hot-dog cart with a striped umbrella (invented brand on the umbrella and the cart), boiled franks, onions, the steam.
- Pretzel and knish cart; Italian-ice/lemon-ice cart (summer); peanut and cashew roaster cart.
- Fruit stand at a corner grocer's sidewalk display, and a flower stand.
- Ice-cream truck (narrative activity exists, adult customers only) and a soft-pretzel man with a basket.
- Newsstands (block A props) staffed by a vendor with papers, magazines, cigarettes and candy.
- Shoeshine stand (chair or box), at Penn, Grand Central and the hotels.
- Peddlers: watches from a coat lining, umbrellas when it rains, "I ♥"-style tourist trinkets with an
  invented slogan, records and 8-tracks on a blanket, sunglasses.
- Times Square / the Deuce: three-card monte hustlers with lookouts (a scam; the police scatter them).
- Garment District: a coffee-and-roll cart for the workers at dawn.
- Grand Central concourse and Penn: shoeshine, a news kiosk, a florist.

## Behaviour (sim)
- Placement by block type and time: hotspot corners, park edges, subway mouths, theatres, the garment district at dawn.
  Carts arrive in the morning and leave at night (pushed, or towed by a van). Weather changes them (umbrellas in rain).
- Queues of customers (ambient adults buy, eat on the go, and toss wrappers). Vendors call out (barks), make change, and argue.
- Reactions: gunfire makes them flee or duck behind the cart; they shout at reckless drivers; police move
  unlicensed peddlers on; monte crews bolt when a cop appears.

## Interaction (gameplay)
- Buy food and drink: small health or stamina bumps plus a short "eating" animation; prices in 1974 money.
- Buy items from peddlers (umbrella, sunglasses, a fake watch for a mission); shoeshine as a tiny ambient action.
- Rob the till (heat via witnesses), knock over carts in chases and with cars (physics props, spilled goods),
  and play three-card monte (rigged, unless you catch the trick).
- Side content: vendors as tipsters, protection-racket beats, permit fights.

## Owners
- **Sim:** archetypes, schedules, placement, queues and reactions. **Gameplay:** buying, robbing, monte, the economy.
- **Vehicles:** carts as physics props (pushable, knock-over, spill), the ice-cream truck and the coffee van.
- **Characters:** vendor wardrobes, work animations (serve, make change, call out, push cart, shine shoes, deal cards).
- **Narrative:** names, barks, cart and umbrella copy, side quests, image prompts. **Audio:** cart calls, sizzle, bell, crowd.
- **Engine:** cart placement slots on sidewalks, prop LOD and instancing, steam particles.
