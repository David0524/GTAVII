# ICT-FaceKit (USC Institute for Creative Technologies): MIT License

Used for: the baked head of lead_delphine (lead_delphine.bin and its textures). The head is a synthesised identity (hand-chosen coefficients on the ICT-FaceKit Light identity modes), never fitted to any real person. Textures are our own procedural work. Source: https://github.com/ICT-VGL/ICT-FaceKit
